- GAME RULES -

  • Players take turns rolling two dice.
  • The sum of the dice is added to your score instantly.
  • Click "PASS" to secure your current score and end your turn.
  • If you roll a "1" on either die, you lose all points accumulated this turn, and your turn ends.
  • If you roll "Snake Eyes" (double 1s), your entire game score resets to 0, and your turn ends.
  • The first player to reach 30 points or more wins!
